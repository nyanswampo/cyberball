Online ball-tossing game

In this part of the study you'll play a short ball-tossing game with two other participants who are online at the same time as you.

The game is a mental visualisation exercise. How well you play doesn't matter. What matters is that you picture the experience in your mind: who the other players are, where you are playing, and what it feels like to be there.

When the ball comes to you, click or tap one of the other players to throw it to them. The game ends by itself after a few minutes.

Please stay on this page until the game has finished. Don't reload or close it, because the game can only be played once.
